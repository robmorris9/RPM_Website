Career overview

Fourteen years across banking systems

I've spent 14 years working across banking operations, markets, treasury, risk, and analytics. At Citi, I led teams in FX and derivatives before moving into liquidity management and market risk. Today at KeyBank, I work across Business Banking portfolio management, credit risk strategy, analytics, governance, and automation.

My work usually sits between the data and the operating model: identifying portfolio signals, turning them into decisions, and building the processes and controls that make those decisions stick. That has included everything from settlement transformation and balance-sheet optimization to credit-line strategy, portfolio monitoring, automation, and risk governance.
