Operating thesis

Finding structure in ambiguous business problems

I'm an operator who looks for structure in ambiguous problems. Across 14 years at Citi and KeyBank, I have led teams close to the transaction, worked on markets and balance-sheet decisions, and built portfolio processes where data, judgment, controls, and execution have to stay connected.

The common thread is not a bank function or a particular tool. I learn how an organization works, find where a decision needs more structure, and build a clearer way to make and carry it out. Analytics, process design, and automation matter when they make that system faster, more reliable, or easier to govern.

At KeyBank, that currently includes credit risk-rating and line-freeze automation, exposure management, a completed Regulation B process automation, and early-stage revolving-line-to-term work with Product and Fair Lending. Banking is where I built this operating pattern; it is not the ceiling on where I can apply it.

Selected work

Four examples of the pattern in practice

01KeyBank·Active initiative

Credit risk-rating and freeze automation

Risk actions on credit cards and revolving lines need to keep pace as relationships change. The work is turning rating downgrades and line-freeze decisions into a repeatable process with clear rules, ownership, exceptions, procedures, and monitoring.

Result

A governed, repeatable workflow for consistent risk action—currently in development.

RoleDesigning the automation, decision rules, and governance; the initiative has no final outcome metric yet.

02Citi·Completed

SWIFT savings through direct settlement

A client's settlement setup relied on a third party, creating network costs that were easy to miss at the headline-process level. A product-processor review surfaced the opportunity, followed by the sustained client and operational work required to establish direct SWIFT settlement.

Result

Approximately $29M in annual network settlement savings.

RoleI identified the opportunity and drove the client migration through implementation.

03Citi·Completed team effort

Balance-sheet response during the 2023 crisis

During the 2023 regional-banking crisis, deposits and HQLA had to be repositioned entity by entity to strengthen liquidity and keep more funding within Citi's US entities. The work required close coordination with international Treasury teams, including Dubai and Hong Kong.

Result

Roughly $10B optimized; roughly $5B able to remain within Citi's US entities.

RoleCore, hands-on contributor to the team effort—not sole owner of the result.

04Across Citi and KeyBank·Cross-career portfolio

Other enhancements

Across roles, I have looked for recurring work that could be made cleaner and more reliable. The individual changes were often small, but built around the people who had to run and trust the process.

  • Spreadsheet enhancements
  • Commentary automation for repetitive P&L pushes
  • Daily and manual upload automation
  • Verification and QC automation

Result

Cumulatively, these improvements saved hundreds of hours while making recurring outputs more consistent and easier to verify.

RoleWork I built or directly improved across multiple roles; the time saved is cumulative, not a single-project claim.
